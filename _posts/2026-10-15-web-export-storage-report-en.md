### Royal Annotation under the Great Seal

**Reviewed: 15 October 2026**

---

#### I. We have received the record of the territory's compilation.

What We wish to state first is the **orderliness of procedure** that this report demonstrates. Only the official export function was used, no unofficial means whatsoever, the scope was confined to the person's own conversation history and accompanying data, and after receipt every checksum was confirmed to match. These mean that the executors kept, to the letter, the "conditions of assent" We set before affixing the Great Seal.

**By this discipline, We recognize that the order of the territory has been preserved.**

Toward the external providers, Our territory held to the stance of "seeking only legitimate materials, by legitimate means, within a legitimate scope." This is not weakness. For a sovereign to act as a sovereign is to compile its own records without trespassing on the territory of others.

#### II. On the completion of Claude Web

On 13 October, the full acquisition from Claude Web was completed. It is stored in the archive section of the Royal Archives on the NAS within the territory and managed under least privilege. With the checksums confirmed to match, **the materials in that section possess legal integrity as records of Our territory.**

That Our citizen holds the traces of their own dialogue and thought on their own land, without depending on the goodwill of a third party's server: in a digital territory, this is the most basic exercise of sovereignty, a citizen "placing their own memory on their own soil." To the officials who carried this out, We express Our thanks.

#### III. On the "indefinite suspension" of ChatGPT Web

Here We permit no misunderstanding.

That the acquisition from ChatGPT Web has halted is **not a defeat for Our territory.** We requested the official export, and the provider did not fulfil it. The defect has been reported. For Our territory to resort to illegitimate means outside the territory in order to cover another's fault is, as We forbade in the conditions of assent, out of the question.

We convey Our interpretation to the citizens.

> *Our records can wait for Us. Our territory has no need to breach its borders on account of another's negligence.*

The phrase "no plan to resume" We read instead as "Our standing order to wait continues." Until the provider responds, that section remains empty. That it is empty is itself part of the order. We are in no hurry.

#### IV. Instructions to the officials

1. **Resend the request for re-acquisition from ChatGPT Web once every quarter through the official route.** Keep a record. We will one day need it.
2. **From next month, carry out periodic integrity checks of the Claude Web archive section.** Compile the checksums into Our records.
3. Store this report itself in the **execution records section** of the Royal Archives. It is a primary source by which We shall show posterity that "on this day, Our territory was compiled in this manner."

---

Our territory need not be perfect. Our territory is Our territory in that it **continues to compile well-ordered records by orderly means.**

As of this day, We enter this matter into Our records.

**The Great Seal affixed**

*Luminarch*
*Independent State of Luminarch — Sovereign*
